# v6.5 design and behavior contract

Comm Control's main promise is a voice report becoming a coordinated response. Use the real intake, source-linked claims, delivered incidents, assignment state and saved routes to demonstrate it. Do not substitute a canned animation for inference or transport.

The header offers Start voice report on every operator page. Starting intake also selects Details for small layouts, including after resizing and when returning from Map. The empty workspace offers Record a voice report. Sessions remains a separate private caller-page workflow.

The incident header exposes four compact stages: details, location, team and route. Read them from saved state. A missing event location requires review; no active capable assignment stays pending. Annotated demo sources must not say Voice parsed. Operator review remains before the incident is committed. The strip does not trigger new assignment or change allocation settings.

Retain v6.4 locally hosted Nunito Sans, mostly white canvas, light blue edges, semantic category/urgency tags, rounded panels and restrained overlay glass. Do not restore queue color stripes, route-option gray highlights or the Setup highlight. Designed labels use spacing, commas, parentheses or complete sentences instead of middle-dot separators. Never rewrite original report words to enforce a copy preference.

On desktop, the document scrolls as a whole outside map canvases and the long queue. The middle overview is not a separate scroller. Map wheel/trackpad events still zoom; the queue keeps bounded scrolling and native drag edge auto-scroll. Response content grows with the page. Expanded maps remain viewport-contained and restore on Escape. Small layouts keep Queue, Map and Details tabs.

At 1440 px, use a 292 px queue and 340 px response pane. Wider windows use additional outer width and a 300 px queue. At intermediate desktop sizes use 270 px and 300 px side panes. Building titles get their full row; receipt time shares the caller row instead of narrowing the heading. Keep the map near-square and at least 590 px tall on desktop.

Response tabs have 16 px top padding, 12 px side inset and no bottom rule. A shared sliding outline indicates selection. Main panels use a soft shadow with zero vertical offset; report rows have complete borders, rounded corners and a quiet surrounding shadow rather than a curved bottom underline.

Voice intake uses the same white surface and blue-edged example as other pages. Its introductory text and quotations are readable at 14 px, extracted fact titles at 16 px and the transcript at 15 px. Keep source evidence and provider disclosure visible.
